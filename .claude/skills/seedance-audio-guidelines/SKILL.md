# Seedance-2.0 Audio Guidelines

## 1. Prompt Guidelines

PE guidelines to reduce native audio generation blocked by audio copyright policy with Seedance-2.0 video generation.

### 1.1 Prompt Structure

- **To increase model's adherence to generating non-copyright content:**
  Add to the prompt: *"Ensure all music, visuals, characters, logos, brands, and video elements are fully original, generic, copyright-safe, and free from any identifiable copyrighted or legally protected references."*

- **For scenarios where BGM is not required:**
  Add to the prompt: *"Do not generate background music."*
  This preserves human dialogue and sound effects while preventing the system from auto-generating background music, enabling more flexible audio layering.
